## The five findings, and the rule they share

Three QA rounds found five real issues, **none of which any automated gate would have caught**:

1. **A live D7 write breach** — three `authenticated`-executable DEFINER *write* doors took
   `app.can_read_case` as their only authority, which S7 satisfied. Proven by *doing it*: pre-cut,
   the reviewer filed a real correction request. The assertion that looked like the D7 pin
   (`308` 1.4, `not can_write_case_content`) was **vacuous** — no admitting door consults that bit.
2. **A Class-2 identity path** (`professional_profiles`, Rule 12/D5) open, returning zero rows only
   because the one profile-bearing participant happened to sit on the locked case — a **data
   coincidence, not a boundary**.
3. **A UI-only interview suppression** over a DB that was open (Rule 1 inversion).
4. **An incomplete family cut** — M10 closed "7 tables routing `can_read_interview`", but the family
   is *things owned by an interview*: `case_interview_links_select` (carrying `external_url`, the
   audio location, not a storage object so the bytes cut never governed it) and
   `can_read_attachment`'s `'interview'` arm reached the same data via raw `can_read_case`. **And the
   guard could not catch it** — `311` §5.1 counted a hardcoded list, so a member never in the list
   could never red it.
5. **A load-bearing invariant that existed only as prose** — "every content source except S7 also
   confers deliberation", which M8, M9 and M10 all rest on.

**The standing rule this phase produced** — recorded in ADR 0100, in the plan at the exact spot that
was wrong, and in the migration that fixed it:

> **Conferring a capability bit requires enumerating its CONSUMERS, not just its producers.**

Plan §A.2's threading list enumerated arms that must *change*; it had no axis for who already
*consumes* the bit being conferred. Findings 1, 3 and 4 are that single mistake in different clothes.
**Phase B (the org_admin content wall) is the much larger instance** — it subtracts a bit from a
principal consumed by far more surfaces than S7 ever was.

**Corollary, from finding 4:** *a guard whose boundary is a literal list cannot close a family —
derive the set, assert over the derivation, and twin it against the empty set.*
